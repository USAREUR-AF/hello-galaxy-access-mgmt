What's under the surface: the pre-cut slot pool

The fabric is brokerless — a Zenoh router moves messages peer-to-peer with no central broker. That buys scale and resilience, but the router's access-control list (ACL) is static: read at startup, not safely hot-editable per tenant under live traffic. So how do you manage identities dynamically? You don't make the ACL dynamic — you pre-cut a pool of identity slots into it up front. Each slot is a fixed 32-hex anchor; its write grant is keyed on the certificate Common Name being equal to that anchor; and the grant follows the slot, never a particular leaf. That one decision is what makes rotate and revoke cheap.
